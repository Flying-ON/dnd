About

While Dungeons and Dragons is technically a game, it feels more like a creative medium. As a group, we build a story together, figuring out how to collaborate, cooperate, and problem solve along the way. Although the Dungeon Master is in charge of creating the world, the players and their characters ultimately are the ones to drive the story.
